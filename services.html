GCC / MOFA Approved

Overseas Employment Medical Checkup

Planning to work in a GCC / Gulf country? JB Medical Center is an approved medical examination center for overseas employment. Bring the following to your appointment:

  • GCC medical slip
  • Original passport
  • 2 copies of passport-size photo
  • Previous medical report (if any)
  • Medical check-up fee
  • GCC online registration form copy

Report and original passport are returned together at delivery.
