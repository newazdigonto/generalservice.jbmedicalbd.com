Our Story

General Healthcare, Built Around You

JB Medical Center is a general medical center based in Khan Complex, Uposhohor Main Road, Sonarpara, Sylhet. We bring together general physicians, medicine specialists, gynaecologists, and diagnostic services — pathology, X-ray, and sonology — under one roof.

We're also an approved center for GCC / overseas employment medical examinations, helping patients preparing to travel for work complete their medical checkup smoothly, from registration to report delivery.
